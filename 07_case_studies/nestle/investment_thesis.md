# Nestle Investment Thesis

This thesis is based on synthetic learning inputs from the lab notebooks. It is not investment advice.

## Company View

Nestle is treated here as a mature, global consumer staples company with resilient revenue, stable margins, strong cash conversion, and defensible brands. The case rests on predictable free cash flow rather than rapid growth.

## Bull Case

- Revenue growth remains steady through pricing power, brand strength, and category breadth.
- Free cash flow conversion stays high because capital expenditure needs remain manageable.
- Defensive business quality supports a lower WACC than more cyclical companies.
- Relative valuation remains supported by premium consumer staples peers.

## Bear Case

- Commodity inflation or foreign exchange pressure weakens margins.
- Terminal value dominates the DCF, making valuation sensitive to small WACC and growth changes.
- A high-quality business may already be fully priced by the market.
- Changing consumer preferences or regulatory pressure could reduce long-term growth.

## Valuation Summary

The synthetic base DCF indicates value above the illustrative current price, while peer multiples create a useful market cross-check. The conclusion should be expressed as a range because terminal value and WACC assumptions drive a large share of the result.

## Decision Framing

The case supports a constructive but cautious stance: quality appears strong, but the investment decision depends on whether the market price offers enough margin of safety.
